# PART 1 — PHOTOS

This is the biggest gap. **13 photo slots are empty**, and right now they show a "Photo coming
soon" tile.

### Please read this first

The photos we have are phone snapshots at 480 pixels wide. That's small. It's why the big
banner image at the top of each page looks slightly soft — we blurred it deliberately, because a
small photo stretched across a full screen looks worse than a blurred one.

**Shoot at the highest resolution your phone or camera allows.** Modern phones do this by
default. Don't send screenshots, don't send photos through a text message if you can avoid it
(texting compresses them badly). Email them, AirDrop them, or upload to Google Drive.

# PART 2 — INFORMATION

## From ownership

**12. Drive time and mileage from the airport** ⭐ *highest impact item on this list*
- From the Sunport terminal to 2500 Yale Blvd SE
- And from the rental car center
- Just drive it once and note the odometer, or pull it off Google Maps
- Why: "1.8 miles, 5 minutes from the terminal" works far better in Google searches than
  "minutes away," and it's what travellers actually search for

**13. A short paragraph about the property**
- Who owns it, how long you've had it, why Yale Blvd, what you want the block to become
- Three or four sentences is plenty
- This replaces placeholder text, and it's the part of the site most likely to get picked up by
  local press and food writers

**14. The salon's opening date** — even "this fall" is better than what's there now

**15. A domain name** — the site is currently at a free github.io address. Worth buying a proper
domain (something like yalelandingabq.com) **before promoting the site anywhere.** Changing the
address later means starting over with Google.
